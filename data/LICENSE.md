# Licence of the data in `data/`

The files in `data/` are an adaptation of the voraus-AD dataset:

> J. T. Brockmann, M. Rudolph, B. Rosenhahn and B. Wandt, "The voraus-AD Dataset for Anomaly
> Detection in Robot Applications," *IEEE Transactions on Robotics*, vol. 40, pp. 438–451, 2024,
> doi:10.1109/TRO.2023.3332224. Rights holder: voraus robotik GmbH.

voraus-AD is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0
International licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Its
NonCommercial and ShareAlike terms carry over, so the material in `data/` is released under
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as well: non-commercial use only, with attribution, and adaptations under the same
licence.

`data/evidence/` (records, unread-channel statistics, payload torque) and `data/kb/certificates.json`
are copied from the SAFEPROCESS 2027 repository
(https://github.com/chenxuanzhou/Structural-Non-Isolability-in-Knowledge-Based-Robot-Fault-Diagnosis,
commit 438389f), where they are released under the same licence.

The raw dataset file is not redistributed.
